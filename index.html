Why equations break when you copy from ChatGPT

When ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek or Claude write mathematics, they use a text code called LaTeX. On the AI website that code is drawn as neat fractions, roots and integrals. When you copy it into Word, you often get raw code such as \frac{a}{b}, missing symbols, or messy formatting. MathToWord fixes this in your browser, so your assignment looks professional.

What MathToWord does for you

  • Shows a clean preview of every equation so you can check it before you paste.
  • Keeps headings, bold and italic text, bullet and numbered lists, tables and code blocks.
  • Creates a .docx file where equations are real Word equations that you can click and edit.
  • Lets you copy a single equation or all equations at once.
  • Runs fully in your browser. Your text is never uploaded.

Who uses it

Students writing math, physics, chemistry or engineering assignments, teachers preparing worksheets, and researchers writing a thesis all face the same problem: AI gives correct maths, but Word will not accept it cleanly. This tool removes that step. Read our how-to guide, the ChatGPT to Word guide, the LaTeX cheat sheet or the page on fixing Word equation errors.
