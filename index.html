Why ChatGPT equations break in Word

If you've ever asked ChatGPT, Gemini or DeepSeek for a formula and pasted it into Word, you know what happens. An equation that looked perfect in the chat turns into a line of backslashes and brackets, such as rac{a}{b}. AI tools write math as LaTeX and draw it with a web library. Word has its own equation format and can't read that web drawing.

What this free equation to Word converter does

MathToWord reads the LaTeX in your text, shows how every equation will look, and then turns it into a Word equation. You can paste math equations to Word in two ways: copy them straight across, or download a Word file in which each formula is already a real, editable equation. Headings, bold words, lists and tables from the AI answer are kept too.
