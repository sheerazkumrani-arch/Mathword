Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions people ask most about moving AI-written maths into Word.

Why do ChatGPT formulas not show properly in Word?

ChatGPT writes math as LaTeX text and draws it with a web library. Word can't read that web drawing, so it pastes either the raw code or something that looks wrong. MathToWord converts the LaTeX into Word's own equation format, so the formula shows up properly.

What is the best free equation to Word converter?

That depends on what you need. If you have equations from an AI chat and want them in Word, MathToWord is built for exactly that and costs nothing. For complete LaTeX documents with packages and tables, a tool such as Pandoc is a better fit.

How do I convert equations to Word?

Paste your AI answer into the box on the home page, check the preview, then click Download Word file. Open the file in Word and the equations are already real Word equations. You can also copy a single equation and paste it into a Word equation box with Alt + =.

Will headings, bold text and lists stay as they were?

Yes, as long as the text still has its Markdown marks, such as ## before a heading and ** around bold words. Clicking the Copy button under the ChatGPT answer keeps those marks. Selecting text with the mouse often drops them.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Anything that writes LaTeX: ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Copilot and Grok. If equations sit between backslash-brackets or dollar signs, MathToWord will find them.

What are AI generated equations in LaTeX?

LaTeX is a text code for maths. Instead of drawing a fraction, it writes the instruction for one. AI tools use it because it works as plain text and can be drawn on any screen.

Copy for Word did not work for me. What now?

Clipboard handling differs between browsers and Word versions. Use Download Word file instead. It builds a .docx with real equations and works in Word 2016 and newer, and it also opens in Google Docs and LibreOffice.

Do you store my text or equations?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent to our server.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free at the moment. If paid features are added later, the basic converter will stay easy to use and any change will be explained on the site.

An equation shows in red. What does that mean?

The LaTeX has a mistake, usually a missing bracket. Ask the AI to rewrite that one equation, or compare it with the LaTeX cheat sheet.
