Frequently asked questions

Why did my equations look broken in Word before?

Copying the rendered preview from a web page sends styled HTML to Word, which cannot turn it into equations. Copying the LaTeX into a Word equation box works because Word converts it itself.

Which AI tools are supported?

Any tool that writes LaTeX, including ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek and Claude.

Which delimiters are detected?

\[ ... \], \( ... \), $$ ... $$ and $ ... $.

Is my text saved?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
