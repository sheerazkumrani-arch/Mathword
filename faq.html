Frequently asked questions

Why did my equations look broken in Word?

AI websites show equations as styled web graphics. When you copy normally, Word receives raw LaTeX code or styled HTML that it cannot turn into equations. MathToWord converts the content into a format Word understands, either on the clipboard or inside a .docx file.

Which AI tools does it support?

Any tool that writes LaTeX, including ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Copilot and Grok. If the answer contains equations written between \[ \], \( \), $$ $$ or $ $, the tool will find them.

Will headings and bold text be kept?

Yes, when the AI text contains Markdown such as ## headings and **bold**. The easiest way to get that is to click the Copy button under the AI answer instead of selecting text by hand.

What if Copy for Word does not work?

Some browsers and Word versions handle the clipboard differently. Use Download Word file instead. It creates a .docx with real equations and works in Word 2016 and newer, and also in Google Docs and LibreOffice.

Are my text and equations saved?

No. Everything is processed in your browser and nothing is uploaded to our server.

Is MathToWord free?

Yes. It is free today. Paid options may be added later for advanced features, and we will always say so clearly.

An equation shows a red error. What now?

The AI probably wrote invalid LaTeX. Ask the AI to rewrite that equation, then paste it again. See our guide on fixing Word equation errors.
