How to copy a ChatGPT answer and paste it into Word

There are three ways to copy an answer from ChatGPT, Gemini or DeepSeek. They give different results, so choosing the right one saves a lot of time when your answer contains maths.

Best method: click the Copy button under the answer, paste into MathToWord, then click Download Word file.

Method 1: The Copy button (recommended)

Under every ChatGPT answer there is a row of small icons. The first one, two small squares, copies the whole answer as text with simple marks: ## for headings, ** for bold text and \[ \] around equations. MathToWord reads these marks and turns them into real headings, bold text and equations. This is the cleanest and fastest method.

Method 2: Select with the mouse

You can drag over the answer, press Ctrl + C and paste. The text arrives, but the marks for headings and bold may be missing, and equations can arrive as plain text. If you use this method, check the preview carefully before you continue.

Method 3: Copy one equation at a time

After pasting into MathToWord, click Copy # next to a single equation. In Word, press Alt + =, then Ctrl + V and Enter. This is useful when you only need one formula inside a document you already wrote.

Why math equation copy paste behaves so differently

The button and the mouse hand over different things. The button gives text with simple marks, the mouse gives a styled web page. Word handles the first through a converter and the second not at all, which is why two copies of the same answer can end up looking so different.
