How to make ChatGPT equations editable in Word

Sometimes the equation lands in Word and looks fine, but you can't change it. It's a picture, or plain text styled to look like maths. For an assignment that your teacher may check or you may need to correct, you want editable math equations, the kind Word itself creates.

How to tell if an equation is real

Click once on the formula. If a grey box appears around it and a new Equation tab shows up at the top, it's a real Word equation. If you only see a picture frame or normal text, it isn't, and you can't edit it as maths.

The easy way: download a Word file

Paste your AI answer into MathToWord, check the preview and download the Word file. The equations are written in the Word equation format, so they behave like ones you'd typed in yourself. You can change a number, add a term or restyle them from the Equation tab.

The manual way for one formula

  1. Copy the LaTeX of the equation (the Copy # button does this).
  2. In Word press Alt + = to open an equation box.
  3. Paste with Ctrl + V.
  4. Press Enter. Word converts the code into a proper equation.

Turning plain mathematical text into editable equations

If your text isn't LaTeX but something like x^2+3x/2, Word can often handle it directly. Open an equation box with Alt + = and type the formula. After a space, Word changes x^2 into a power and a/b into a fraction. This works for simple formulas. For anything long, LaTeX plus the converter is safer.

About the Word equation format

Word offers professional (the nicely stacked layout) and linear (one-line) display. You can switch between them from the Equation tab if a formula looks too tall or too flat. Both are still editable.

If something looks wrong, read the fix guide. To learn the shortcuts, see the Word equation editor guide.
