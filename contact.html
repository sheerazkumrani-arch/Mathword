We read every message. Replies may take a day or two. For quick questions, check the FAQ and the page on fixing Word equation errors first, because most problems have a simple fix.

Suggest a feature

Do you want support for another file type, another language or another AI tool? Write to us. Features requested by many students come first.

Report a wrong result

If an equation in the preview looks different from the one on the AI website, copy the exact text that you pasted and send it to us. Seeing the original text lets us find the cause quickly and improve the converter for everyone who uses it after you.

Business and collaboration

Teachers, institutes and content creators who would like to work with us, share the tool with students, or request a custom version are welcome to write by email or WhatsApp. Please include a short description of your idea and the number of students who may use it.
