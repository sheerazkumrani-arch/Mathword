Fix equation formatting in Word: ChatGPT formulas not showing

If your ChatGPT formulas are not showing correctly in Word, the cause is usually one of a handful of things. Find the one that matches what you see.

You see backslashes and curly brackets

You pasted LaTeX into normal text. Word only turns LaTeX into maths inside an equation box. Press Alt + = first, paste, then press Enter. Or skip all that and use the Download Word file button in MathToWord.

The equation is squashed, shifted or aligned wrongly

Delete the messy lines and bring in the content again through the download or Copy for Word. Equation formatting in Word depends on how the formula was inserted, and a clean insert gives you a centred formula on its own line.

Headings and bold text are gone

This happens when text is selected with the mouse. Use the Copy button under the AI reply instead, so the ## and ** marks come along. The converter turns them into real headings and bold words.

A formula is red in the preview

Red means the LaTeX itself can't be read, often because of a missing brace or a stray backslash. Ask the AI to rewrite that one equation. If you want to find the problem yourself, compare it with the LaTeX cheat sheet.

Strange double backslashes

Sometimes a copied answer has doubled backslashes, like \\( and \\pi. MathToWord repairs this automatically in most cases. If a formula is still red, reduce the double backslashes to single ones or ask the AI for a fresh version.

Copy for Word does nothing

Some browsers block clipboard access, and some Word versions ignore what is copied. Try Chrome or Edge, or use the download.

Old versions of Word

Equations from the download work best in Word 2016 and newer. Very old versions may not show every symbol. Google Docs and LibreOffice can open the file as well.
