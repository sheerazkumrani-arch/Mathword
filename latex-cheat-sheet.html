LaTeX cheat sheet for Word equations and math symbols

You don't need to learn LaTeX to use MathToWord. Still, knowing a few commands helps you read what the AI wrote and fix small mistakes. These are the ones students use most.

Building blocks

You writeYou get
\frac{a}{b}Fraction a over b
\sqrt{x}Square root of x
x^2 and x_1Power and subscript
\int_0^1 f(x)\,dxDefinite integral
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}Sum to infinity
\lim_{x\to 0}Limit

Word mathematical symbols in LaTeX

Greek letters are \alpha, \beta, \theta, \pi, \sigma and \omega. Comparisons are \leq, \geq, \neq and \approx. Common signs are \pm, \times, \cdot and \infty.

Brackets and spacing

Use \left( ... \right) so brackets grow with tall content such as fractions. \quad gives a wide space and \, a thin one, for instance before dx.

Matrices

A matrix looks like \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}. The & separates columns and a double backslash starts a new row.

Mistakes that cause red errors

  • A missing closing brace is the most common reason.
  • Without the backslash a command becomes plain text.
  • Words inside a formula need \text{ like this }.
