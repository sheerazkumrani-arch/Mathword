How to paste AI math equations into Word

AI tools write equations in LaTeX, for example \frac{a}{b}. Word understands LaTeX inside its equation box, so the trick is to paste into that box and not into normal text.

  1. Paste the AI answer into the tool on the home page.
  2. Check the preview. Every equation should look correct.
  3. Click Copy beside one equation.
  4. In Word press Alt + = to insert an equation box.
  5. Press Ctrl + V, then Enter. The equation becomes editable.

Common problems

  • Equation shows as plain text: you pasted outside an equation box. Press Alt + = first.
  • Red error or missing symbols: the AI wrote invalid LaTeX. Ask the AI to rewrite that equation.
  • Old Word versions: LaTeX input needs Word 2016 or newer (Microsoft 365 recommended).
