How to copy ChatGPT math equations into Word

Getting math from an AI chat into a Word assignment should take seconds. This guide shows the three ways MathToWord helps, from the quickest to the most reliable. All three keep your equations correct and keep the layout of your answer.

Step 1: Copy the answer from the AI

In ChatGPT, Gemini or DeepSeek, click the small Copy button under the answer. This copies the full text including headings, bold markers and equations. Selecting the text with the mouse also works, but headings and bold style may be lost.

Step 2: Paste it into MathToWord

Open the home page, click the left box and press Ctrl + V. The preview on the right updates automatically. Check that every equation looks right.

Step 3: Move it into Word

  1. Copy for Word: click the blue button, switch to Word and press Ctrl + V. This is the fastest way.
  2. Download Word file: click the green button and open the .docx. Equations are real Word equations, so you can click and edit them. Copy the content into your own assignment if needed.
  3. One equation only: click Copy # next to it, press Alt + = in Word to open an equation box, paste with Ctrl + V and press Enter.

Tips for a clean assignment

  • Use Word 2016 or newer. Microsoft 365 gives the best equation support.
  • After pasting, set your font and size from the Home tab as usual. Equations use Cambria Math automatically.
  • If the AI mixes text and equations on one line, use the inline option: keep the equation between \( and \).
  • Always read the maths again. AI can make mistakes, and your teacher will mark the answer, not the tool.
