How to copy math equations into Word

This page takes you from an AI chat to a finished Word file. The first time it may take a few minutes. After that it's about thirty seconds.

Step 1: Copy the answer from ChatGPT

Under every reply there's a row of small icons, and the first one looks like two overlapping squares. Click it. That copies the whole answer together with the marks that show which lines are headings and which words are bold. You can also drag with the mouse and press Ctrl + C, but then headings and bold often get lost on the way.

Step 2: Paste it into the box on the home page

Click the left box on the home page and press Ctrl + V. The preview on the right draws every equation the way it will look in Word. Read through it before you move on. If one equation is red, it has a typo in its LaTeX. The fix guide explains how to deal with it.

Step 3: Paste equations into Word

There are three ways to get the result into your document:

  1. Download Word file. This is the most reliable one. Open the downloaded file in Word. Every equation is a real Word equation, so you can click it and change a number or a sign. Copy the parts you need into your assignment.
  2. Copy for Word. This is quicker because you skip the file. On some versions of Word it pastes perfectly, on others it doesn't. If the result looks wrong, use the download instead.
  3. Copy one equation. Click Copy # next to the formula, press Alt + = in Word to open an equation box, paste with Ctrl + V and press Enter.

When the download is the better choice

If your answer has many equations, a table or several headings, download it. One file keeps everything in order. Copying equation by equation is fine for a single formula inside a document you've already written.

Small habits that save time

  • Ask the AI to write equations in LaTeX. Most do by default.
  • Read the maths again after pasting. AI can get a step wrong, and Word won't warn you.
  • Set your font and size from the Home tab afterwards. Equations use Cambria Math automatically, which is normal.

If you're stuck, try the copy methods guide or send us a message.
