About MathToWord

MathToWord was created by Sheeraz Khan to solve a problem every student faces today: AI tools such as ChatGPT give correct maths, but copying it into a Word assignment breaks the equations and the formatting. Hours are wasted retyping symbols by hand.

Our goal

We want students, teachers and researchers to move AI-written maths into Word in a few seconds, with the same look as on the AI website. Headings stay headings, bold stays bold, and equations stay real, editable Word equations.

How it works

The tool reads the LaTeX in your text, shows a clean preview, and then either copies formatted content for Word or builds a .docx file. All of this runs inside your own browser, so your homework is never uploaded.

Free, and improving

MathToWord is free today. We plan to add more tools for students, such as other document formats and extra guides. Your feedback decides what we build next, so please tell us what you need.

Important note

AI can make mistakes in maths. Always check your final answers yourself before submitting any assignment.

Who it is for

Secondary and university students, teachers who prepare worksheets and exam papers, and researchers who write reports or a thesis. Anyone who uses ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek or Claude for mathematics, physics, statistics, chemistry or engineering can save time with this tool.

Our promise

We keep the tool simple, fast and private. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not keep what you paste. When we add new features, we will explain clearly what changes and what stays free.
