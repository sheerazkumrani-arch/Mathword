About MathToWord

MathToWord is a small free tool made by Sheeraz Khan for a problem that students run into daily. AI tools give correct maths, and then Word refuses to take it. Hours go into retyping fractions and symbols by hand, usually right before a deadline.

Why it exists

While working on assignments with ChatGPT, the same pattern kept repeating: copy, paste, and a mess of backslashes. Every converter that could fix it asked for payment or a sign-up. This one is free and runs in your browser, because that seemed fairer for students.

What it does

You paste AI generated equations and the tool shows them cleanly. Then it either copies the content for Word or creates a Word file where each equation is a real, editable equation. Headings, bold text, lists and tables from the AI answer come along as well.

Who it's for

School and university students, teachers preparing worksheets and papers, and researchers writing reports or a thesis. If you use ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek or Claude for maths, physics, statistics, chemistry or engineering, it will probably save you time.

What it isn't

It doesn't check your answers. AI sometimes writes a wrong step in perfect LaTeX, so read the maths before you hand anything in. It also doesn't convert whole LaTeX documents, and the LaTeX page explains what to use for those.

Feedback is welcome

New features come from what people ask for. If something doesn't work in your version of Word, tell us what you saw and we'll look into it.
