Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains in simple words what MathToWord does and does not do with your information.

Your text stays with you

All equations and text you paste are processed inside your own browser. We do not send them to our server, store them or sell them. When you click Download, the file is built on your device.

Third-party libraries

To work, the site loads open-source libraries (KaTeX, JSZip and mathml2omml) from public content delivery networks. Those providers may see your IP address as part of normal web traffic.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We use Vercel Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly service, to count visits and see which pages are popular. We may also show ads through Google AdSense in the future. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings, and you can block cookies in your browser at any time.

Children

This site is educational and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to sheerazkumrani@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can stop using the site at any time, clear your browser data, or use private browsing. Because we do not store your pasted text, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If we add new services such as analytics or advertising, we will update this page and change the date below, so you can always see how your information is handled.
